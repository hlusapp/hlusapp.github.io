# Data Deletion Request — BT AutoPlay

**App:** BT AutoPlay
**Developer:** BT AutoPlay

## Overview

BT AutoPlay does not require you to create an account. Most data (selected Bluetooth devices, player preferences, playlist links, local diagnostic log) is stored **only on your device** and is deleted automatically if you:

- clear the app's data (**Settings → Apps → BT AutoPlay → Storage → Clear Data**), or
- uninstall the app.

## Data Stored on Our Server

The only information transmitted to our server is:

- a hashed (SHA-256) identifier derived from your device;
- the start date of your free trial period;
- your purchase status (purchased / not purchased).

This data is **not linked** to your name, email address, or any account, and cannot be used on its own to identify you personally. It is used solely to correctly track the trial period and prevent it from being repeatedly restarted through reinstallation.

## How to Submit a Deletion Request

To request deletion of the record associated with your device from our server:

1. Email us at **hlusapp@gmail.com** with the subject line: **"Data Deletion Request — BT AutoPlay"**.
2. In your message, state that you wish to delete the record associated with your device.
3. Since the identifier is hashed and not linked to a name or email address, we cannot look up your record automatically — we will reply with instructions on how to retrieve your device's identifier so we can confirm which record to delete.

Once confirmed, the record will be deleted from our server within **30 days**.

## Important Notes

- Deleting this record does **not cancel** or **refund** any purchase already made through Google Play. Purchases are managed directly by Google Play in accordance with their [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- If you reinstall the app after your record has been deleted, the trial period will start over, since the record of your previous trial will no longer exist.
